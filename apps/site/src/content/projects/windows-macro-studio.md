Macro Studio is a Windows desktop application for recording, editing, and replaying keyboard, mouse, and controller actions. Create reusable macros, assign hotkeys, organize profiles, and automate repeated clicks, key presses, or scrolling. Your recordings and settings stay on your computer; no account is required.
