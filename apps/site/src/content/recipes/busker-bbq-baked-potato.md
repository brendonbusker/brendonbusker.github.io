<h2>Busker BBQ Baked Potato</h2><h3>Ingredients</h3><ul><li><p>1 lb (454 g) ground beef</p></li></ul><ul><li><p>2 cloves garlic, minced (8 g)</p></li><li><p>120 g BBQ sauce (about ½ cup)</p></li><li><p>30 g ketchup</p></li><li><p>15 g Worcestershire sauce</p></li><li><p>1 tsp smoked paprika (2 g)</p></li><li><p>1 tsp chili powder (2 g)</p></li><li><p>½ tsp garlic powder (1 g)</p></li><li><p>½ tsp onion powder (1 g)</p></li><li><p>½ tsp black pepper</p></li><li><p>½ tsp kosher salt (adjust to taste)</p></li><li><p>Optional: ½ tsp chipotle powder or cayenne for heat</p></li></ul><h3>Instructions</h3><ol><li><p>Bake potatoes until fluffy (400°F / 205°C for 60–75 minutes).</p></li><li><p>Brown the beef over medium-high heat.</p></li><li><p>Drain excess grease if needed.</p></li><li><p>While it cooks add all the seasonings, garlic, and sauces to a bowl and mix</p></li><li><p>After the beef is cooked, add the sauce mixture and cook for another 1-2 minutes.</p></li></ol><p>You want it thick enough that it sits on top of the potato instead of running off.</p><hr /><h2>Build the Potato</h2><p>Split open a baked potato and fluff the inside with:</p><ul><li><p>1 tbsp butter</p></li><li><p>Salt &amp; pepper</p></li></ul><p>Then top with:</p><ul><li><p>BBQ ground beef</p></li><li><p>Sharp cheddar cheese</p></li><li><p>Sour cream or plain Greek yogurt</p></li><li><p>Green onions</p></li><li><p>Crispy bacon</p></li><li><p>Chives</p></li></ul><p>Optional extras:</p><ul><li><p>Jalapeños</p></li><li><p>Crispy fried onions</p></li><li><p>Pickled red onions</p></li><li><p>Sauteed red onions</p></li></ul><img alt="bbq busker potato" data-layout="block" src="/uploads/recipes/busker-bbq-baked-potato/1791569895273-299dd06a-485d-4f29-9190-61f69049b90a.webp" /><p></p>
