Preheat your oven to 425°F.</strong></p><p>Line a baking sheet with parchment paper or lightly grease a baking dish.</p><p><strong>2. Arrange the chicken.</strong></p><ul><li><p>Remove the chicken thighs from the refrigerator.</p></li><li><p>Arrange them in a single layer.</p></li><li><p>Spread them out so they aren't overlapping.</p></li><li><p>Leave the marinade coating the chicken, but avoid pouring excessive liquid into the pan.</p></li></ul><p><strong>3. Prepare your potatoes.</strong></p><ul><li><p>Wash the potatoes thoroughly.</p></li><li><p>Leave the skin on or peel them.</p></li><li><p>Dice into approximately <strong>¾-inch (2 cm) cubes</strong>.</p></li><li><p>Rinse the diced potatoes under cold water to remove excess surface starch.</p></li><li><p>Dry thoroughly using paper towels or a clean kitchen towel.</p></li></ul><p><strong>Important:</strong> Getting the potatoes dry is one of the biggest factors in achieving crispy potatoes.</p><p><strong>2. Finish with Parmesan (optional).</strong></p><p>Immediately after removing the potatoes from the air fryer, toss them with 20 g grated Parmesan.</p><p>The residual heat will help the cheese adhere to the potatoes.</p><p>You can skip this if you prefer.</p><hr /><h2>Estimated nutrition per serving</h2><p>Assuming everything is divided evenly into four meals, with the Parmesan included:</p><table><tbody><tr><th colspan="1" rowspan="1"><p>Macro</p></th><th colspan="1" rowspan="1"><p>Per serving</p></th></tr><tr><td colspan="1" rowspan="1"><p><strong>Calories</strong></p></td><td colspan="1" rowspan="1"><p><strong>~645 kcal</strong></p></td></tr><tr><td colspan="1" rowspan="1"><p>Protein</p></td><td colspan="1" rowspan="1"><p>~57 g</p></td></tr><tr><td colspan="1" rowspan="1"><p>Carbohydrates</p></td><td colspan="1" rowspan="1"><p>~53 g</p></td></tr><tr><td colspan="1" rowspan="1"><p>Fat</p></td><td colspan="1" rowspan="1"><p>~25 g</p></td></tr><tr><td colspan="1" rowspan="1"><p>Sodium</p></td><td colspan="1" rowspan="1"><p>~1,350–1,500 mg</p></td></tr></tbody></table><p>These are approximate values, particularly because some marinade and cooking fat may remain in the baking dish.</p><h3>Suggested portions</h3><p>For each of your four meals:</p><ul><li><p><strong>250 g raw chicken thighs</strong> (weigh before cooking)</p></li><li><p><strong>225 g raw potatoes</strong></p></li><li><p>Optional: 100–150 g broccoli, asparagus, or green beans</p></li></ul><p>With a vegetable, you're looking at approximately <strong>680–700 calories and 60 g protein</strong> per meal.</p><p><strong>One final recommendation:</strong> Because the honey chipotle chicken is relatively bold and sweet-spicy, I wouldn't add any more honey or sweet sauces to the potatoes.
