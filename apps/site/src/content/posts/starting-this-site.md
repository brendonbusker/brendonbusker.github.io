I wanted a corner of the internet that could hold more than a polished list of finished things. This site is also for the notes, experiments, and half-formed questions that happen along the way.

Projects tend to look inevitable once they are done. The useful parts—the awkward first version, a good constraint, the decision that quietly changed everything—are easy to lose. Keeping a dated record is my way of holding on to those details.

The site itself is part of that work. It is intentionally quiet: writing first, projects when there is something worth showing, and a résumé that reads like a document instead of a download prompt.

I expect it to change as I do. That is the point.
