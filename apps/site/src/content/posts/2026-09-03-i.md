<p>Recently I deleted TikTok.</p><p>This came after a long realization that I wasting my time mindlessly scrolling when I could be spending that time doing more important things like gaming and watching anime (and ofc spending time with my wife 🤣).</p><p>To some people this might seem like some obvious conclusion that TikTok is a waste of time, but I bet some think my reasoning for doing it sounds backwards.</p><p>To fully explain my thought process let me tell you a common story that would happen to me, and I'm willing to bet at least a few people can relate.</p><p>I'd start my day out as usual, wake up, kiss the wife goodbye and go to work. After spending a whole day working, I'd think about some of the things I'd like to do with my free time.</p><p>Maybe I'd want to watch a show I've put off recently, or maybe I'd want to play a game that I've been thinking about, or maybe I'd like to simply sit on Discord and talk to some buddies I haven't heard from in a while.</p><p>I'd yearn for things like this and when it finally came time to do these things, I'd consistently just not do them.</p><p>This happened for a while until I finally realized that what I was doing was scrolling mindlessly for hours. I'd tell myself while scrolling, "just five more minutes and I'll get off." Of course, I never get off, not in 5 minutes at least.</p><p>This would go on long enough to the point that when I'd look at the time it was way too late to start any of the things I actually wanted to do.</p><p>And the worst part? I couldn't even tell you what the reel I watched 5 minutes ago was even about.</p><p>So that was it. I decided that I quite literally wanted to focus more on gaming and anime, and so I deleted the app that was holding me back.</p><p>The result? I've watched a bunch of Dragon Ball Z and I've played a bunch of Pokemon Emerald.</p><p>Just check how many Gold Symbols I have now 😎</p><img alt="emerald-symbols" data-layout="block" src="/uploads/posts/i/1788465818913-2a92290f-691e-4687-a2a6-3335e3f65988.webp" /><p>I don't think everyone needs to straight up delete TikTok, I've met plenty of people who can handle themselves fine with the distraction and still do the things they want to do.</p><p>But for me, I realized I literally turn into a drone when I'm using that app and I just can't let it keep controlling me anymore.</p>
