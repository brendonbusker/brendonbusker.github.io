<p>I’ve just had the most wonderful weekend with my family celebrating Labor Day. We did water sports on a boat, hung out in the pool and played many board games.</p><p>Although vacation time is awesome in every way, there is always one thing that it seems to lack.</p><p>There is no consistent way to train.</p><p>I really should just be relaxing with my family, but I still don’t want to get fully behind on all my gains I’ve been getting back recently. So I came up with a bold new technique to keep myself accountable during vacation.</p><p>I have put a price on peeing.</p><p>Whenever I have to go to the bathroom I make myself do 25 push ups after every single time.</p><p>I didn’t really count, but I estimated that in 2 days I did close to 500 push ups, which was pretty crazy considering how easy it was.</p><p>I really only spent like 15 more seconds just doing it really quick after peeing, and I practically had a pump all day. </p><p>It definitely got harder throughout the day, but since the time between pees was always pretty long it wasn't really too difficult to pump out 25.</p><p>Now that I’m home writing this post I don’t really feel like I’ve lost any gains.</p><p>That being said, I certainly didn’t really gain any muscle either as intermittent push ups aren’t hardly a replacement for a real 1 hour focused training session, but I can feel it: I am leagues better off than if I just did nothing.</p><p>To those reading, I encourage you to try something similar, and I don’t think it needs to be exactly what I’m doing.</p><p>Maybe you want to do situps instead, or maybe you want to do 10 pushups, whatever works really.</p><p>I think what’s most important is the fact you make a self-rule and stick to it.</p><p>It's kind of like making your bed in the morning, no one else but you is going to keep you accountable for doing it (except wifey lol). </p><p>I think it's very important people have some sort of self-rule in their lives to keep them growing either physically, mentally, spiritually or all of the above.</p><p>Life is really about doing the hard things even when no one else is around to see it. No one will know if I really did those 25 pushups after peeing but me.</p><p>What works for me, might not work for you but I do think this specific self-rule has been both fun and rewarding.</p><p>So the next time you find yourself going to pee, you should ask yourself:</p><p>"Should this really be for free?"</p>
